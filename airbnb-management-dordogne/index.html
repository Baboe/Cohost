The Dordogne areas I actually cover

Bergerac is the base, but the coverage runs wider — out toward Sarlat, Eymet, Lalinde, Issigeac, Périgueux and the villages between them. Each town pulls a different kind of guest and needs a different read on pricing. Pick your area below, or ask directly if you don't see it.

Different towns, one person who knows the difference

A rental near the Bergerac airport doesn't behave like one in the Périgord Noir tourism economy, and a bastide with a British expat community doesn't need the same listing copy as a market-day village that empties by lunchtime. Coverage isn't a radius on a map — it's knowing what each town actually offers a guest.
